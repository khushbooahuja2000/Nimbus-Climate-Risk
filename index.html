Transition risk: the cost of the shift to a low-carbon economy

Version 1 measures the biggest and most certain transition risk for a cement maker: the cost of India’s carbon rules. Other transition risks are listed at the bottom of this page as a watch list.

Carbon rules: measured

India’s Carbon Credit Trading Scheme sets each cement plant a CO₂-per-tonne target against an FY2023-24 baseline. Plants that miss it must buy carbon credit certificates. No certificate has traded yet, so every price here is an assumption you can change.

Assumption Nimbus is fictional, so it has no notified target. Default reflects the reported cement range of about 7%.
